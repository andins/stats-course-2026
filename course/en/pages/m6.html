Module 6: Building Models

Once you know why you're modeling (Module 5), there are a handful of practical concerns that apply almost regardless of the specific model.

Pages

  1. Overfitting and regularization — why a model that fits your data perfectly can still be a bad model.
  2. Cross-validation and model checking — how to tell if a model is good.
  3. Multicollinearity — what happens when your predictors overlap too much.
  4. Measurement error — what happens when a predictor or outcome is a noisy proxy for what you actually meant to measure.
