# Module 2: Exploring Your Data

You have data. Before running any test, there is a question to answer and a first step to take.

## Pages

1. [Exploratory vs. confirmatory analysis](./exploratory-vs-confirmatory.md) — are you testing one pre-decided idea, or hunting for patterns?
2. [EDA basics](./eda-basics.md) — why looking at your data (literally, with plots) always comes first.
3. [Missing data](./missing-data.md) — what to do about the gaps.

## Why this comes before hypothesis testing

Jumping straight to "which test should I run" skips a more important question: is this analysis even the kind where a classical test's guarantees apply? Most real-world data exploration is messier and more open-ended than the textbook "one test, one hypothesis" story — and that changes what's appropriate.
